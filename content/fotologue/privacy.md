**Last updated:** November 2026

## The short version

Fotologue collects no personal data. There is no account system, no analytics SDK,
no advertising identifier, and no crash reporting that transmits your information to us.

## What stays on your device

Rolls, frames, exposure notes, darkroom records, and any images you import live entirely
on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no server copy because
there is no server.

## Network access

Fotologue requires no network access to function.

## Permissions

Fotologue requests only the permissions its features require, listed in-app at first use.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the change is noted in the release notes.
