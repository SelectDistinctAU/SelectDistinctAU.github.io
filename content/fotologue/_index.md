**Fotologue** (*Foto* + *Catalogue*) is a high-precision, privacy-first Android application
that bridges physical film shooting, wet darkroom craftsmanship, and lifetime digital
archiving.

<!-- Verify every claim below against the app before publishing. -->

## Why use it?

- **Nothing leaves your device.** No account, no cloud sync, no telemetry.
- **Built around how film actually works.** Roll and frame numbering, not an infinite
  scroll of digital files.
- **Darkroom-aware.** Development times, dilutions, dodging and burning notes — recorded
  against the exact frame they belong to.
- **Lifetime archive.** Export everything so your negatives stay legible decades from now.
