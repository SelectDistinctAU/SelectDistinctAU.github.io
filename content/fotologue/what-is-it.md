---
title: "What is Fotologue?"
description: "The idea behind the app, in plain terms."
---

Most photographers using film still carry a notebook. You write down the frame number, the
aperture, the shutter speed, what you were hoping for — and then lose the notebook, or
can't read your own handwriting in the darkroom three weeks later.

Fotologue replaces that notebook with a structured, searchable catalogue that travels with
you. It turned out digital artists needed the same discipline, so it works for them too.
