We create Android apps that help people navigate life with less stress and more clarity.
No data harvesting, no hidden agendas — just honest, useful software built with care.

## When we build

We build when we see a gap — when a tool doesn't exist, or when existing tools feel like
they're working against you, not with you.

We build when we can make a difference — even a small one.

## Our apps

### [Fotologue](/fotologue/)

A photographic catalogue suite — equally empowering all painting-with-light artists.
Replace the notebook: record the scene, the settings and the purpose as you shoot.
Built for analogue photographers; adopted by digital artists doing the same.

*In testing.*

### [Distinct Routes](/distinct-routes/)

A safety-first navigator. While other apps focus on getting you there quickly or cheaply,
we focus on getting you there — and back — as safely as possible. Powered by BBBike and
OpenStreetMap.

*In preparation for release.*
