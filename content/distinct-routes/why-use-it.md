---
title: "Why use Distinct Routes?"
description: "Who it's for and what problem it solves."
---

- **Riders with wheels other than two narrow ones** deserve routes that fit them.
- **Safety-first ranking** — quieter roads, better surfaces, fewer hazards.
- **Open data** — built on OpenStreetMap and BBBike, not a walled garden.
