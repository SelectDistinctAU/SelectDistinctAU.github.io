---
title: "What is Distinct Routes?"
description: "The idea behind the app, in plain terms."
---

Most routing apps optimise for the fastest path or the shortest distance. For a cargo bike
hauling two children, a handbike, or a mobility scooter doing 8 km/h, "fastest" usually
means "six lanes of traffic and a hill you cannot climb."

Distinct Routes lets you choose what safety means for *your* vehicle and body.
