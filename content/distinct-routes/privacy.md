**Last updated:** November 2026

## The short version

Distinct Routes collects no personal data and has no account system, analytics SDK,
advertising identifier, or crash reporting that transmits your information to us.

## What stays on your device

Favourites, ride history, saved trails, and GPX exports live entirely on your device.
Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no server copy because there is no server.

## Network access

Distinct Routes fetches map and routing data from BBBike and OpenStreetMap services when
you plan a route. These requests necessarily carry your approximate route endpoints —
that is how routing works. We do not receive, store, or correlate these requests.
