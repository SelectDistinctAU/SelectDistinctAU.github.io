**Distinct Routes** plans safety-focused routes prioritised for calm backstreets, quiet
cycleways, flat terrain and greenways — rather than high-speed motorways. Powered by
BBBike and OpenStreetMap.

<!-- Verify: vehicle profiles and speed caps below against the app before publishing. -->

## Why use it?

- **Vehicle profiles that match reality** — standard bicycle, e-bike, cargo bike, tandem,
  wheelchair, handbike and mobility scooter.
- **Preferences you control** — green routes, avoid unlit streets, rough surfaces, ferries,
  highways and steep hills.
- **Zero cloud tracking** — ride journal and favourites stay on your device.

## A necessary disclaimer

This app does **not** guarantee route safety. It surfaces routing data. Riders and mobility
device operators remain responsible for assessing local conditions before setting off.
