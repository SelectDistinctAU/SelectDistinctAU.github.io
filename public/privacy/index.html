Privacy Policy

How Select Distinct handles your data. Short answer: we don't.

Last updated: November 2026

The short version#

Neither Fotologue nor Distinct Routes collects personal data. There is no account system, no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, and no crash reporting that transmits your information to us.

What stays on your device#

App Data held locally
Fotologue Rolls, frames, exposure notes, darkroom records, images you import
Distinct Routes Favourites, ride history, saved trails, GPX exports

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no server copy because there is no server.

Network access#

Distinct Routes fetches map and routing data from BBBike and OpenStreetMap services when you plan a route. These requests carry your approximate route endpoints, which is unavoidable for routing to work. We do not receive or store these requests.

Fotologue requires no network access to function.

Permissions#

Each app requests only the permissions its features require, listed in-app at first use.

Changes#

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the change is noted in the release notes.

Contact#

Questions, or want to report a concern confidentially? SelectDistinctAU@pm.me — anonymous is fine.
