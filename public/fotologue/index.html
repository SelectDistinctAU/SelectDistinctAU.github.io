Fotologue

A Photographic Catalogue Suite — equally empowering all painting-with-light artists.

Fotologue (Foto + Catalogue) is a high-precision, privacy-first Android application that bridges physical film shooting, wet darkroom craftsmanship, and lifetime digital archiving.

Why use it?#

  • Nothing leaves your device. No account, no cloud sync, no telemetry.
  • Built around how film actually works. Roll and frame numbering, not an infinite scroll of digital files.
  • Darkroom-aware. Development times, dilutions, dodging and burning notes — recorded against the exact frame they belong to.
  • Lifetime archive. Export everything so your negatives stay legible decades from now.

In testing.

What is Fotologue?

Most photographers using film still carry a notebook. You write down the frame number, the aperture, the shutter speed, what you were hoping for — and then lose the notebook, or can’t read your own handwriting in the darkroom three weeks later. ...

Why use Fotologue?

Film shooters — log frame, lens, film stock, filters and intent before you forget them. Darkroom printers — development times and notes pinned to the exact frame. Digital artists — keep provenance and purpose alongside the work. Everyone — your records stay on your device. Always.
