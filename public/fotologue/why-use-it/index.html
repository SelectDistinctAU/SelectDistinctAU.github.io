Why use Fotologue?

Who it's for and what problem it solves.
  • Film shooters — log frame, lens, film stock, filters and intent before you forget them.
  • Darkroom printers — development times and notes pinned to the exact frame.
  • Digital artists — keep provenance and purpose alongside the work.
  • Everyone — your records stay on your device. Always.
